# Narration IR: plan what each sentence means, then animate

short film `short-narration-ir`, 139 s as cut. Machine transcript of Will's recorded take (lower case, recognition errors kept), one sentence per line.

- Before an AI writes the animation code that you can see visually in my videos, it writes a plan and that includes what each sentence means
- as well as in terms of timings what each change in the picture should be at the times in which I say words.
- And so this is a short summary of narration IR which is stands for intermediate representation
- and these are the instructions I made for writing those plans one of a series of short companions to my longer videos.
- And so to summarize, here's a plan for one sentence from my spin glass video.
- Now turn only the third arrow down.
- So the plan says arrow 3 turns from up to down and arrow 1, 2 and 4 stay up
- and the highlight starts on the third and the turn is finished by down.
- So each plan quotes its sources and a checking program that comes with narration IR and confirms each quote is in the source file
- and after I record each movement is timed to my recorded words
- and the second command prints everything for one sentence
