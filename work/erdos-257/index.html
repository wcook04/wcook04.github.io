Erdős #257

Erdős asked whether adding infinitely many of the fractions 1, 1/3, 1/7, 1/15 and so on always gives a total that cannot be written as a fraction. He stated one case in 1968 and proved it with an extra restriction. Here that case is proved without the restriction, and Lean accepts the proof.

Stated limit: The question Erdős asked, for every infinite choice, is still open. The papers are written with AI and are not peer reviewed.

Golomb proved one case in 1963 and Erdős stated more in 1968. Google DeepMind's Formal Conjectures lists the 1968 case as solved.
