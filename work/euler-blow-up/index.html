Euler blow-up

Their work
Levent Alpöge, Tristan Buckmaster and Matei Coiculescu wrote the paper and its Lean proof. Their AI statement says they used Claude and Codex throughout, and they thank Anthropic for supporting the collaboration.
My work
I built a reader where clicking a phrase of their paper opens the Lean that states it, with a comparison of the two, a map of the argument, and an adapted edition where AI added explanations to their text with every change marked.

Alpöge, Buckmaster and Coiculescu construct smooth periodic initial data for the three-dimensional incompressible Euler equations whose solution blows up in finite time. The velocity stays bounded while the vorticity at the origin becomes infinite.

Limits: The links between sentences and Lean, the titles and the comparisons were made with AI, and no mathematician has reviewed them. The adapted edition’s added explanations are AI-written too, and every change is marked.

Lean is a program that checks mathematical proofs.
